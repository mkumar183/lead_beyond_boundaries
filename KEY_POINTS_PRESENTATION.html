Lead Beyond Boundaries

Training Effectiveness — Team 6

How a leadership workshop measurably changed how we lead AI adoption across Digital AgTech

Feb 2026 workshop → May 2026 shipped outcomes

The Problem: Adaptive, Not Technical

  • AI adoption at scale is not a tool rollout problem — it requires identity, habit, and belief shifts
  • AI makes coding easy and fast — but at production scale the time to finish doesn't fall much; review, testing & vigilance eat the gains, and the tech isn't mature enough to trust unchecked
  • Spans 9 India products across 4 product groups with multiple scrum teams each
  • Senior engineer resistance is identity protection, not Luddism — craft built over years feels threatened
  • Industry benchmark: 78% of enterprises have AI pilots; under 15% reach production
  • Goal: Prove the training changed how Team 6 leads, using AI adoption as the test case

Solution: Pilot-Then-Cascade + Guardrails First

  • Pilot-then-cascade: Prove deep adoption in one squad; let social proof spread
  • Guardrails before usage: Published Coding Agents Policy (one tool per team) to remove ambiguity
  • Co-author with skeptics: Loudest skeptics in each squad own the boundaries they defend
  • Quality over throughput: Measure rework reduction & cycle time, not "% engineers logged in"
  • Cross-silo learning: Brown Bag forum (3 delivered, 8 scheduled) shares what worked and what didn't

How the Workbook Shaped Leadership Moves

Heifetz: Adaptive vs Technical Named AI adoption as an adaptive challenge; stopped treating it as a tooling rollout.
Loyal Soldier → Growth Partner A mindset and cultural shift: stopped waiting for briefs, started anticipating blockers and influencing by design. Built a culture where people speak up and drive change.
VALUE Framework V: Connect to grower outcomes. A: Catch "pilot-becomes-permanent" early. L: Reframe away from "efficiency." U: Audience-match language. E: Remove "is AI OK here?" ambiguity.

Cultural Listening & Accountability

Hofstede Framework

High-power-distance, uncertainty-avoidance, collectivism culture. Strategy: Identify Invisible Influencers per squad; publish guardrails first to remove ambiguity; structure Brown Bag to surface failure content, not just success stories.

Three Levels of Listening

Leaders drove adoption by listening past the stated objection. At Level 2 the real fear surfaced — "Will my manager think I didn't do the work?" — and a Level 3 question ("what would your AI guidelines look like?") turned skeptics into co-authors instead of conversion targets.

Challenges & How We Addressed Them

  • Identity threat: Reframed as "leveraging AI effectively is a new skill and way of working" rather than "AI is more efficient"
  • Shifted bottleneck: Coding got fast, but testing, review & deploy did not — the constraint moved downstream, so we invested across the whole SDLC
  • Loyal Soldier defaults: Safe to wait, report up, polish before sharing. Requires deliberate, repeated practice to shift
  • Pilot-to-production gap: Requires structural practice (Brown Bag, agent cadence, guardrails), not just intent
  • Hallucination & accountability: Clear chain: engineer → EM → sponsor. AI never on the accountability chain
  • Metrics pressure: Resisted "% logged in" in favor of quality & rework metrics — harder to count, more meaningful

Outcomes: What We Shipped

  • Vulnerability Sentinel (Planting) — Agent parses AWS Inspector + Dependabot, patches dependencies, validates, raises PR in one automated run
  • Sonar-fixer Agent (Sustainability Mobile) — Auto-fetches SonarCloud issues, fixes, runs tests, raises PR (PR #1347 shipped)
  • Agent-discussion cadence — Recurring cross-functional practice (Chitra + Ram + Ravi) producing a pipeline of agents
  • AI Integration Experiment (Sahil Sharma + Josh Larson, Seeds) — Measured throughput step forward; Manoj's written endorsement: "real value from AI adoption at scale"

Measured Results: The Data

Agrobiodiversity: AI for Speed. Humans for Correctness.

React Native v77 upgrade: From ~12 story points / 12–16 days to 3–4 SP / 3–5 days. 67–75% reduction in time & points. 8–9 SP freed for other work. Code quality maintained independent of speed.

Test Quality Benchmarked

Naive AI test generation: 78% coverage, 30% false positives — "never use." Human-led AI three-stage: 82% coverage, 5% false positives — "best." The squad explicitly chose the safer path.

Scale caveat: these gains come from a small, contained experiment, where change is easy to direct. Reproducing them across many teams is a far larger undertaking — it needs broader thinking, shared processes, and cultural and structural change.

Institutionalized Practices (The Real Outcome)

  • Brown Bag forum (Akshay coordinates): 3 sessions delivered, 8 scheduled through October 2026. Cross-silo learning with "what didn't work" explicitly on agenda
  • Coding Agents Policy: One tool per team removes ambiguity; published before usage to build psychological safety
  • Shared vocabulary: "Loyal Soldier → Growth Partner," "Invisible Influencer," "anticipate and ask" — makes invisible patterns nameable across the team
  • Accountability clarity: Portkey LLM guardrails, 8-pillar RFC-009 framework, human-in-the-loop gates. Engineer → EM → Sponsor; AI never accountable

Not a tool. A shared vocabulary that makes the invisible visible.

"Loyal Soldier → Growth Partner." From waiting for briefs to anticipating blockers.
"Anticipate and Ask." The question we were afraid to ask.
"Invisible Influencer." The person whose quiet adoption is the unlock.

Two agents in production. One operating principle published. One cadence recurring. One practice, not a one-off.
