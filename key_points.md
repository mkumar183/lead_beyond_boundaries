# Key Points — Lead Beyond Boundaries: Training Effectiveness (Team 6)

## Problem Statement / Objective
- Prove that the *Lead Beyond Boundaries* leadership training (Feb 2026, facilitated by Nandan / Purple Cow Consulting) measurably changed how Team 6 leads — using AI adoption across Digital AgTech Pune as the test case.
- AI adoption at scale is an **adaptive challenge**, not a technical one: it requires identity, habit, and belief shifts across 9 India products and multiple scrum teams — not just a tool rollout.
- The use case directly aligns to Uday's 2026 Engineering Priority #1: Flagship Agentic Engineering (code vulnerabilities, unit coverage, E2E test coverage).

## Solution
- Adopted a **pilot-then-cascade** model: prove deep adoption in one squad, let social proof spread — rather than mandating a uniform rollout.
- Applied workbook tools deliberately: VALUE framework, Stakeholder Map, Trust Model, and Three Levels of Listening to shape every leadership decision.
- Shifted from measuring "% engineers logged in" to measuring quality, rework reduction, and cycle time.
- Published guardrails (Coding Agents Policy: one tool per team) *before* pushing adoption to remove ambiguity.
- Co-authored usage guidelines with the loudest skeptics in each squad so they own the boundaries.
- Stood up a cross-team **Brown Bag forum** (Akshay coordinates; 3 sessions delivered, 8 scheduled through Oct 2026) to share what worked *and* what didn't.

## How Workbook Principles Shaped the Solution
- **Heifetz adaptive-vs-technical frame** — named AI adoption as an adaptive challenge; stopped treating it as a tooling rollout problem.
- **VALUE framework** (Visualise → Anticipate → Lead from Purpose → Understand Context → Enable) — each letter produced a specific leadership move: connecting adoption to grower outcomes (V), catching "pilot-becomes-permanent-pilot" early (A), reframing away from "efficiency mandate" (L), audience-matching language (U), removing the "is AI OK here?" ambiguity (E).
- **Loyal Soldier → Growth Partner mindshift** — stopped waiting for briefs; started anticipating blockers, sharing imperfect drafts, and influencing by design.
- **Hofstede** (high-power-distance, uncertainty avoidance, collectivism) — identified Invisible Influencers per squad; published guardrails first; structured Brown Bag to surface failure-content, not just success stories.
- **Trust Model + Three Levels of Listening** — listened at Level 2 to what engineers actually feared ("will my manager think I didn't do the work?"); involved skeptics in co-authoring guardrails rather than attempting conversion.

## Challenges
- **Senior engineer resistance** — not Luddism but identity protection; craft built over years feels threatened. Reframed as "AI mastery is the new craft" rather than "AI is more efficient."
- **Loyal Soldier defaults are hard to break** — waiting for the brief, reporting up, polishing before sharing all feel safe; requires deliberate, repeated practice to shift.
- **Pilot-to-production gap** — industry-wide, 78% of enterprises have AI pilots but under 15% reach production. Staying out of that 85% requires structural practice, not just intent.
- **Hallucination and accountability** — published explicit guardrail layers (Portkey LLM guardrails, 8-pillar RFC-009 framework, human-in-the-loop review gates) and a clear accountability chain: engineer → EM → sponsor; AI is never on the chain.
- **Measuring the right things** — resisting pressure to report throughput metrics (% logged in) in favour of quality and rework metrics, which are harder to count but more meaningful.

## Outcomes
- **Two agents shipped to production** across two products: Vulnerability Sentinel (Planting, CWPT-3935) and Sonar-fixer (Sustainability Mobile, PR #1347) — directly delivering on Uday's Priority #1 (code-critical vulnerabilities).
- **AI Integration Experiment** (Sahil Sharma + Josh Larson, Seeds): Test 1 produced a measurable throughput step forward; Manoj's written endorsement ("real value from AI adoption at scale") is the strongest single proof point.
- **Agrobiodiversity operating principle codified** by Lalitkumar Sakare — "AI for Speed. Humans for Correctness." — with quantified before/after data: React Native v77 upgrade went from ~12 SP / 12–16 days to 3–4 SP / 3–5 days (67–75% reduction).
- **Recurring Agent-discussion cadence** (Planting team, organised by Chitralekha Ruikar) producing further agents — a practice, not a one-off.
- **Brown Bag forum institutionalised** — cross-silo learning running regularly with "what didn't work" content explicitly on the agenda.
- **Training's largest unmeasured outcome**: a shared vocabulary ("Loyal Soldier → Growth Partner," "Invisible Influencer," "anticipate and ask") that makes previously invisible patterns nameable and discussable across the team.
